Scammers have always pretended to be the boss: "I'm in a meeting, I need you to buy gift cards, keep this quiet." What's new is that AI can now copy a person's **voice** from a short clip, like a voicemail greeting, a video or a conference talk, and fake their **face** on a video call.

In early 2024, a finance worker in Hong Kong paid out about **US$25 million** after a video call where the "CFO" and several "coworkers" were all deepfakes. Everyone on the call looked and sounded real.

## Why it works

These scams don't depend on technology alone. They lean on three pressures:

1. **Authority**: it's someone you don't want to say no to.
2. **Urgency**: it has to happen now, before you can think.
3. **Secrecy**: "don't tell anyone", so you can't check.

When all three show up together, treat it as a scam until proven otherwise, no matter how real the voice sounds.

> [!REDFLAG] The moment to stop
> Any request to move money, buy gift cards, change bank details or share passwords that comes with "do it now" and "keep it between us".

## How to check

- **Hang up and call back** on a number you already have, not one from the message or the caller.
- **Ask in person**, or message them on a different app you already use with them.
- **Agree on a code word** with your team for urgent money requests. A deepfake won't know it.
- **Slow down on purpose.** A real boss can wait five minutes while you double-check.

> [!TIP] It's not rude to verify
> Most managers would much rather get a "just confirming this was you" call than lose the money.

```quiz
Your "CEO" calls from an unknown number. It sounds exactly like her. She needs five $500 gift cards for a client in the next hour and asks you not to mention it to anyone. What do you do?
- [ ] It's her voice, so buy them
- [ ] Buy one card first to test it
- [x] Hang up and call her on the number you already have, or ask her in person
Why: Voices can be cloned. Urgency plus secrecy plus gift cards is the scam pattern. Calling back on a known number is the check a scammer can't fake.
```
