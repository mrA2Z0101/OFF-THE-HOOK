---
title: The fake IT helpdesk
date: 2026-10-20
summary: Scammers call or Teams-message pretending to be IT, then ask you to install remote-support software. Real IT rarely contacts you out of the blue.
tags: phone, coworkers
---

This scam doesn't need a link at all. Someone pretending to be **IT support** contacts you, often right after your inbox has been flooded with spam, and offers to "fix it". They ask you to open a remote-support tool, like **Quick Assist** (built into Windows), AnyDesk or TeamViewer, and give them the code.

Once they're connected, they have your screen, your files and anything you're signed in to. Microsoft has publicly warned about criminals using exactly this trick.

```email
From: IT Help Desk (External) <helpdesk.it@outlook.com>
To: you@yourcompany.com
Subject: (Teams chat) Spam issue on your account
Verdict: phishing

Hi, this is Jason from IT. ==We noticed you're getting a lot of spam today|They often cause the spam flood themselves first== and we're rolling out a fix.

==Can you open Quick Assist and send me the 6-digit code?|Handing over that code gives them control of your computer== It only takes 5 minutes.
```

Notice the **(External)** label: in Microsoft Teams, chats from people outside your company are marked. Your real IT team won't be external, and won't use an `outlook.com` address.

## How to check

- **You call them.** If someone says they're IT, hang up and call your helpdesk on the number you already know.
- **Never share a remote-access code** with someone who contacted you first.
- **Spam flood followed by a "helpful" call** is a known pattern. Report both.

```quiz
Right after a flood of spam emails, someone on Teams says they're from IT and asks you to open Quick Assist so they can fix it. Their name shows "(External)". What do you do?
- [ ] Open Quick Assist: they're trying to help
- [x] Don't respond; call your IT helpdesk on the number you know
- [ ] Ask them for their employee ID first
Why: An outside account asking for remote access is the scam. Calling IT yourself is the check they can't fake.
```
